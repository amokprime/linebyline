## Root cause of v4 paste failure

Your "Typed assertions are all off" observation was spot-on — and the Trash.zip baselines confirmed it. The 0-byte chromium baselines prove paste didn't fire at all on chromium during baseline regen, and the 88-byte typing baselines prove the same (only DEFAULT_META, no paste content).

The bug was in my v4 `page.evaluate`:
```js
await page.evaluate((text) => {
  navigator.clipboard.writeText(text);  // ← Promise NOT awaited
}, readMedia("plain_english.lrc"));
```

`navigator.clipboard.writeText` returns a Promise. Without `await` inside the evaluate, the function returns immediately, and `Control+v` sometimes fired **before** the clipboard was populated.
