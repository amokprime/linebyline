**Why both flags, not just `--proto`:** `--proto` gates the initial request; `--proto-redir` gates redirect hops. `--proto-redir` defaults to HTTP+HTTPS+FTP+FTPS, so `--proto "=https"` alone still permits an HTTPS→HTTP redirect. Literals must be double-quoted — an unquoted `--proto =https` loses the `=` sigil to shell word splitting.
